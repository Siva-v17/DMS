Pricing questions

Before you pick a plan

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle, with no data loss either way.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full feature access and no credit card required to start.

What happens if we exceed our e-signature limit?

Additional signatures are billed at $2 each, or you can upgrade to a plan with a higher or unlimited allowance.

Do nonprofits or education get a discount?

Yes — eligible organizations receive 30% off any plan. Reach out to sales with proof of status.
