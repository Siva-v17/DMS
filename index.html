Common questions

Answers before you ask

How long does migration from our current drive take?

Most teams under 50,000 files finish in under a week using the guided importer, with a specialist assigned on Business and Enterprise plans.

Can external partners view files without a Stackly account?

Yes — share a view-only or comment-only link with expiry and watermarking, no login required on their side.

Where is our data physically stored?

US and EU regions are available on Team and above, with in-country residency add-ons for regulated industries.

Do you support e-signature in multiple languages?

Signing pages currently support 14 languages, with the signer's browser locale detected automatically.
