Our story

Paper never disappeared. We just gave it a better home.

Stackly Vault started in a compliance closet — literally. Three founders, a decade of shared-drive horror stories, and one idea: documents deserve infrastructure, not folders.

How we started

Built by people who cleaned up the mess first

Before Stackly, our founders ran document operations at a hospital network, a regional bank, and a 200-lawyer firm. Each had the same problem: critical files scattered across drives, inboxes, and cabinets, with no single source of truth when an auditor called.

In 2021 they started building the system they'd always wanted — one vault, one search bar, one audit trail. Stackly Vault shipped its first customer contract eight months later.

What we believe

Principles that shape every release

Security is the product

We treat encryption, access control, and audit logging as core features, not an add-on tier bolted onto a file share.

Search should just work

If a document exists in the vault, finding it should never take longer than remembering its name.

Compliance without the friction

Retention, holds, and access reviews should run quietly in the background, not become a project of their own.
