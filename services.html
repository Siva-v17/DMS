What we set up for you

Services that get your documents vault-ready in weeks, not quarters

Beyond the software, our services team handles the migration, the workflow design, and the training so the vault fits how your people actually work.

Guided migration

We map your existing drives, cabinets, and inboxes, then move every file into Stackly Vault with folder structure and permissions preserved.

Workflow design

Our solutions architects rebuild your intake, approval, and retention processes as automated Stackly Vault workflows.

Compliance setup

We configure retention schedules, legal holds, and access policies to match your industry's regulatory requirements from day one.

Team onboarding

Live training sessions and role-specific playbooks get every department productive in the vault within the first week.

API & integrations

We connect Stackly Vault to your CRM, HRIS, and accounting stack so documents attach themselves to the records they belong to.

Ongoing governance

A dedicated success manager reviews access, storage, and audit health with you every quarter.

Engagement model

A team assigned to your rollout, not a ticket queue

Every services engagement starts with a discovery workshop, followed by a phased migration so no department loses access to a file mid-project. Most customers are fully live within 30 to 60 days.

01

Discovery workshop

We audit current storage, workflows, and compliance obligations in a half-day session.

02

Phased migration

Departments move in waves, starting with the highest-risk document types first.

03

Go-live & training

Live sessions per department, recorded for future hires, plus a 30-day hypercare window.
